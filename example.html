Borrow a Camera

Reserve one in about a minute, and pick it up at the desk.

  1. Sign in and select Cameras.
  2. Pick a day and a kit.
  3. Bring your ID to the desk on that day.

See How Long You Can Keep It

Questions?

The desk is open 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. on weekdays.

How Long You Can Keep It

For a Class

Up to a week, with your course number.

For a Club

Up to three days, with your club's adviser.

For Yourself

One day, returned by 5 p.m.
